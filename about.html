About us

35+ years of specialist sealing

AMR Sealants is a dedicated sealant & mastic specialist — the professional mastic man homeowners, builders and businesses across the Home Counties call when they want it done properly.

Our story

Sealing is all we do

Most tradespeople seal as an afterthought. For us, it's the whole craft — and that focus is exactly why the finish lasts.

With more than 35 years of hands-on experience and an NVQ Level 2 qualification, we've sealed thousands of bathrooms, kitchens, wet rooms and commercial spaces. We know which product belongs in which joint, how to prep a surface so silicone actually bonds, and how to leave a bead so clean you'll want to show it off.

From a single mouldy bathroom reseal to a full new-build package, every job gets the same care — tidy work, quality materials and a seal built to outlast the fashion of the room.
