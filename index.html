About AMR Sealants Ltd

The professional mastic man you can rely on

We're a specialist sealant firm — not a general builder who seals as an afterthought. Sealing is all we do, and it shows in the finish.

With over 35 years on the tools and an NVQ Level 2 qualification, we seal new kitchens and bathrooms, cut out and replace tired or mouldy silicone, and handle windows, floors and full wet rooms for homeowners, builders and businesses alike.

  • Trade-grade Mapei, Dow Corning & Ottoseal products
  • Domestic, new build, refurb, office & hotel work
  • Tidy, respectful and fully insured on every job

What we do

Specialist sealant services

Everything from a single bathroom reseal to full commercial mastic packages — applied by hand, finished to last.

New kitchen & bathroom sealing

Crisp, watertight silicone to new bathrooms, kitchens and wet areas — sealed to last and finished to a professional standard.

Mould removal & resealing

We cut out tired, blackened or mouldy silicone, clean the joint back and reseal it so it looks brand new and stays mould-free.

Internal & external windows

Weather-tight mastic sealing to internal and external window frames, keeping out draughts and water for good.

Floors & skirtings

Neat flexible seals where floors meet skirtings and walls — ideal for wet rooms, bathrooms and high-traffic areas.

Showers & wet rooms

Fully sealed showers and wet rooms using premium silicones built to handle constant water and movement.

Commercial & new build

Mastic and sealant work for new builds, refurbishments, offices and hotels — reliable finishes, on schedule.
