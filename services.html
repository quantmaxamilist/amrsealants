Specialist sealant work

Our sealing & mastic services

Whether it's a fresh bathroom, a mouldy reseal or a full commercial fit-out, we bring 35+ years of specialist sealant experience to every job across London, Hertfordshire, Bedfordshire, Buckinghamshire & Oxfordshire.

New kitchen & bathroom sealing

A brand-new bathroom or kitchen deserves a flawless seal. We apply crisp, even silicone beads around baths, basins, worktops, splashbacks and shower trays — perfectly watertight and finished to a professional standard that protects your investment for years.

  • Baths, basins, showers & trays
  • Worktops & splashbacks
  • Premium anti-mould silicones
  • Neat, consistent bead every time

Cutting out & resealing mouldy silicone

Old silicone goes black, peels and harbours mould. We carefully cut it all out, clean and prepare the joint properly, then reseal with fresh trade-grade silicone — so your bathroom looks new again and stays mould-free far longer.

  • Full removal of old/blackened sealant
  • Deep clean & preparation of the joint
  • Anti-fungal, mould-resistant silicone
  • Instantly fresher, cleaner finish

Internal & external window sealing

Poorly sealed windows let in draughts, damp and water. We reseal internal and external window frames with weatherproof mastic that keeps the elements out and helps your home stay warmer and drier.

  • Weatherproof external mastic
  • Neat internal frame seals
  • Draught & water protection
  • Domestic & commercial frames

Floors, skirtings & wet rooms

Where floors meet walls and skirtings, a flexible seal keeps water where it belongs. Ideal for wet rooms, bathrooms and busy commercial floors, our seals flex with the building and stay intact.

  • Full wet room sealing
  • Flexible, long-lasting joints
